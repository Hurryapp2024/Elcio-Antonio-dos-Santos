01 / BUSINESS TRANSFORMATION

Aureon

Website implemented · private access

Connecting a consulting proposition with an interactive institutional website and structured business assessment.

Positioning · Requirements · Customer journey
Read the Aureon case study +

The business challenge

Aureon combines consulting, workflow improvement and digital products. Visitors need to understand that broader proposition and distinguish developing products from services they can discuss.

My contribution

I defined the positioning, requested revisions to the visitor experience, specified assessment fields and reviewed the product presentation. Implementation used AI-assisted development.

DecisionImplementation
Make consulting central to the propositionBusiness Transformation, AI Workflows & Vertical SaaS positioning
Keep the Home focusedA separate institutional About page
Let visitors explore relevant productsFive selectable product presentations with development status
Collect useful contextAssessment form with industry, needs and business challenge
Keep movement optionalReduced-motion support and a motion control

Implementation evidence

The site includes an assessment endpoint and a Cloudflare D1 data model. The website build and version 10 deployment completed on September 13, 2026. Its private access is maintained; this portfolio does not expose customer records.

What remains to evaluate

End-to-end intake testing, device and accessibility review, lead management and assessment completion metrics. No conversion or revenue improvement is claimed.

02 / FIELD OPERATIONS

Track&Go

In development · workflow design

Bringing office planning, field execution and operational visibility into a connected service workflow.

Operations · Service coordination · Business requirements
Read the Track&Go case study +

The operational challenge

Service businesses need to manage changing schedules, assignments and completion updates while retaining visibility into costs and follow-up.

My contribution

I define use cases informed by construction and cleaning operations, map the relationship between planning and execution, and review functionality around daily usability.

Workflow design sample

StageIntended handoff
IntakeA clear customer request, scope and requested timing
PlanningAn assigned visit with instructions and ownership
ExecutionA completion or exception update from the field
ReviewConfirmation, unresolved issues and cost inputs
Financial follow-throughInformation available for billing and analysis

Proposed evaluation

Assignment completeness, timeliness of completion updates, rework frequency and availability of job-cost inputs. These are proposed measures; no production performance results are presented.

This sample describes intended behavior and does not certify that every step is implemented.

03 / COMMERCIAL WORKFLOWS

Converty

In development · product definition

Turning market signals into structured research, account qualification and follow-up priorities.

Market intelligence · Qualification · Commercial planning
Read the Converty case study +

The business challenge

Research volume only becomes useful when an opportunity has a traceable source, a reason to pursue it and a clear next step.

My contribution

I define prospecting criteria, source-tracking requirements, account and contact organization, and follow-up workflows, refining the relationship between research and relevant offers.

Qualification design sample

  • Retain the source, event date and current status.
  • Document the business need and fit with the proposed offer.
  • Distinguish verified facts from unresolved questions.
  • Assign ownership and a next action.
  • Keep review separate from message sending.

Proposed evaluation

Source coverage, documented qualification decisions and follow-up completion would help assess the process. These are design criteria, not a claim of a deployed scoring system or measured conversion results.
