About me

I bring an entrepreneurial perspective to business development, commercial management, and operational improvement. My background spans telecommunications, construction, cleaning services, and business consulting—environments where customer relationships, service delivery, and financial discipline need to work together.

Managing businesses exposed me to a recurring challenge: customer information, schedules, field activities, and financial records often live in disconnected systems. That experience now informs my work on Aureon, an ecosystem connecting business consulting, AI workflows, and digital product development.

My contribution centers on understanding the business problem, defining requirements, shaping workflows, and reviewing whether the solution supports the people who will use it. I use AI-assisted tools in planning, prototyping, and development, with human review and validation as part of the process.

How I work

01 — Understand
Start with the customer, the operating model, and the people doing the work. Examine where information moves, decisions happen, and friction accumulates.

02 — Prioritize
Turn broad challenges into a focused problem statement, desired outcome, and a set of assumptions to evaluate.

03 — Define
Translate the priority into workflows, requirements, or an improvement plan. Consider ownership, usability, cost, and implementation effort.

04 — Review and refine
Use prototypes, implementation reviews, and feedback to identify missing steps and improve the fit between the solution and the business.

05 — Evaluate
Define relevant measures and use operational feedback to determine what should happen next.
